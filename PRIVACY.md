# Privacy Policy — Video QoL

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Video QoL does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

- **Your settings**: the default speed, the per-site speeds and the auto-arm sites you enter on the options page, including the site names you type. They are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, so if Chrome sync is on, Google syncs them between your own browsers like any extension setting. The extension never sends them anywhere else.
- **Nothing about the sites you visit** is recorded: only the sites you add yourself appear in the settings.

## What the extension accesses

- **Video elements on the pages you visit**, to add the control bar, change playback speed, take screenshots and open the pop-out player. This happens entirely in your browser.
- **The visible area of the current tab**, only when you take a screenshot of a video that the site doesn't allow the extension to read directly. The capture is cropped to the video and saved to your downloads; nothing is uploaded.
- **Downloads**, only when you click the download button. The video is downloaded from the same address the page is already playing it from.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
- No accounts, and no servers operated by the extension.
- No reading of page content beyond video elements and on-screen subtitles shown in the pop-out player.
- No remote code: everything the extension runs is included in the package.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's repository or contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
