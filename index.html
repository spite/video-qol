Install

Better controls for every video on the web

Speed up or slow down any video, step through it frame by frame, save the exact frame as a PNG, and keep watching in a pop-out player when you switch tabs. On any site, with the same keys.

Install, it's free View on GitHub

No tracking, no accounts, nothing leaves your browser.

Every player is different

One site stops at 2×. Another hides its speed menu three clicks deep. Hardly any let you step one frame at a time, and none of them save the frame you're looking at.

Video QoL puts the same small bar on every video, with the same keys everywhere, so you stop learning a new player on every site.

Small controls, everywhere

A bar that appears in the corner of the video you hover, and gets out of the way when you don't need it.

Speed

Any speed, from 0.06× to 16×

Press [ and ] to go slower or faster, and \ to flip between 1× and your last speed. It goes well past most players' own limits.

Set a default speed and a speed for each site, and new videos start at it. If a site resets the speed on its own, it's put back.

Frames

Step frame by frame, save the exact frame

Pause and press , or . to move one frame at a time, at the video's real frame rate. Alt+, and Alt+. jump 5 seconds.

Alt+S saves the current frame as a PNG at the video's full resolution, not the size it's shown at.

Pop-out

Keep watching while you do something else

Arm a video and it floats in its own window when you switch tabs, then goes back when you return. Or pop it out right away with Alt+Shift+P.

The window has its own controls: seek bar, volume, speed, screenshot, and fullscreen back in the tab. Subtitles on YouTube and Netflix come along too.

Options

Your speeds, your keys

Speeds per site, sites whose main video pops out on its own, and a hidden bar wherever you'd rather just use the keys. Every shortcut can be changed or turned off.

Settings save as you type and sync with your Chrome profile.

Downloads

Save videos that play from a single file, named after the page. Streamed and protected video is left alone.

Stays out of the way

The bar only shows on the video you hover, fades when you leave, and hides when a menu or popup covers the video.

Works where you watch

In fullscreen, in embedded players, and on sites that rebuild their player as you go.

The same keys on every site

They act on the video you last hovered, or the biggest one on screen, and never while you're typing.

Questions

Does it work on YouTube and Netflix?

Speed, frame stepping, the shortcuts and the pop-out player do. Downloads don't: those sites stream video in small pieces, so there's no single file to save, and Netflix's video is DRM-protected, so its frames can't be captured either.

Why didn't the video pop out when I switched tabs?

Chrome decides when a page may pop out on its own. The video has to be playing with sound, and the site needs Chrome's Automatic picture-in-picture permission, which Chrome grants by default only on sites where you often watch media. If nothing happens, open the site's settings from the address bar and set it to Allow.

A shortcut clashes with a site's own keys.

Change it or turn it off in the options. The frame keys , and . only act on a paused video, so sites that use them while playing keep them.

Can I use the keys without the bar?

Yes. Hide the bar on every site, or only on the ones you list. The keys, the pop-out and the short speed messages keep working.

Which browsers does it work in?

Desktop Chrome, version 116 or later. It doesn't work in mobile browsers.

Something doesn't work on a site.

Players vary a lot, so please open an issue with the site's address. After the extension updates, reload your open tabs once as well.
